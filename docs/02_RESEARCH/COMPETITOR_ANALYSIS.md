## NeuroGuard's intended differentiation

The project is not claiming individual sensor novelty. Its contribution
is the integration of:

**biological rationale + custom electronics + embedded architecture +
haptic feedback + caregiver software + wearable packaging**

## Claims to avoid

Do not claim "first", "only", "clinically proven", or "detects
infection" without systematic evidence and validation.
