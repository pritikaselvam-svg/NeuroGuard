# Literature Review Framework

## Research questions

1.  Which caregiver-relevant behaviors can be measured non-invasively?
2.  Which wearable sensors are appropriate?
3.  Which signals are direct measurements versus indirect indicators?
4.  What algorithms are used for fall/activity/sleep detection?
5.  How are wearable systems validated?
6.  What are the limitations and false-positive sources?

## Evidence categories

### Behavioral

-   Movement intensity.
-   Activity timing.
-   Repeated unusual movement.
-   Sudden acceleration.
-   Night-time activity.

### Physiological

-   Heart rate.
-   Pulse waveform.
-   SpO2-related optical measurements.

### Adherence

-   Reminder acknowledgement.
-   Missed-dose events.

## Review method

For every paper, record:

-   Citation.
-   Population.
-   Sensor.
-   Signal.
-   Algorithm.
-   Outcome.
-   Validation method.
-   Sample size.
-   Limitation.
-   Relevance to NeuroGuard.

## Important distinction

The literature should support the rationale for measuring a signal. It
does not automatically validate NeuroGuard's thresholds or algorithms.
Those require separate testing.
